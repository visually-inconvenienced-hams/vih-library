CONTESTS AND ON-AIR EVENTS — Tuesday, November 17 to Wednesday, December 16

Thirty days of contests and on-air events, in one place. Our net runs every Saturday. This file is replaced with a fresh edition as new dates are added. All times are 24-hour; contest times are Central with UTC in parentheses.

THIS WEEKEND

Visually Inconvenienced Hams Net — Saturday, November 21 at 19:00 Central, on the Blind Hams Bridge. Sweepstakes is an HF contest and does not affect the net.

ARRL Sweepstakes Contest, SSB — Saturday, November 21 at 15:00 Central to Sunday, November 22 at 21:00 Central (21:00 UTC November 21 to 03:00 UTC November 23). SSB on 160, 80, 40, 20, 15, and 10 meters. Exchange: serial number, precedence, your call sign, check, and section. You may operate up to 24 of the 30 hours, and each station counts once. A small log still counts. Logs are due by 21:00 Central on Sunday, November 29 (03:00 UTC November 30).

LZ DX Contest — Saturday, November 21 at 06:00 Central to Sunday, November 22 at 06:00 Central (12:00 UTC November 21 to 12:00 UTC November 22). CW and SSB on 80, 40, 20, 15, and 10 meters, working Bulgarian stations. Exchange for stations outside LZ: signal report and your ITU zone number. Logs are due by 18:00 Central on Sunday, December 6 (00:00 UTC December 7).

Also running this weekend: three 160-meter contests on Saturday, November 21 (the All Austrian 160-Meter Contest, 10:00 to 17:59 Central; the REF 160-Meter Contest, 11:00 to 18:00 Central; and the RSGB 1.8 MHz Contest, 14:00 to 17:00 Central); the NRRL Telephony Contest, with sessions on Saturday, November 21 at 07:00 to 09:00 Central and Sunday, November 22 at 01:00 to 03:00 Central and 07:00 to 09:00 Central; the South American Integration Contest, CW, Saturday at 12:00 Central to Sunday at 15:00 Central; the Feld Hell Sprint on Saturday at 13:00 to 14:59 Central; and the second session of the AWA Bruce Kelley 1929 QSO Party, Saturday at 17:00 Central to Sunday at 21:00 Central. Earlier this week: the Classic Exchange, Phone, with a session running Tuesday, November 17 at 08:00 Central to Wednesday, November 18 at 02:00 Central; the NAQCC CW Sprint on Wednesday, November 18 at 19:30 to 21:30 Central; the NTC QSO Party on Thursday, November 19 at 13:00 to 14:00 Central; and the Walk for the Bacon QRP Contest, with sessions on Wednesday, November 18 at 18:00 to 19:00 Central and Thursday, November 19 at 20:00 to 21:00 Central.
