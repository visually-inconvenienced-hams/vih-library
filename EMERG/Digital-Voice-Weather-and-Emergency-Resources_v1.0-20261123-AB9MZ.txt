Digital Voice Weather and Emergency Resources
By Gregory D. Rosenberg, AB9MZ
Version 1.0. November 23, 2026.

This list has digital voice places where hams talk about bad weather and emergencies. Each entry has three parts: the number and the name on one line, and what it is for on the line under it. The list is grouped by mode: DMR, D-Star, System Fusion, and Other Modes.

Skywarn is the National Weather Service program of trained storm spotters. ARES is the Amateur Radio Emergency Service. RACES is the Radio Amateur Civil Emergency Service. Both are groups of hams who help in emergencies.

What the words mean. A talkgroup is a virtual channel on a DMR network. Radios set to the same talkgroup hear each other. A reflector is a computer on the internet with no radio of its own. It links many repeaters and hotspots so they can all hear each other. A reflector has modules, marked by a letter, like A, B, or C. A room is the System Fusion name for the same kind of link. A conference is the EchoLink name for a link that joins many stations at once. A node is one station or repeater on a network like EchoLink or AllStar. Each node has its own number.

Most entries here are regional. They serve one state or one area. A few are national. To find the ones near you, ask your local National Weather Service office or your local ARES group. Talkgroups and rooms can change. Check the source link before you use one. Some entries come from a BrandMeister list made in 2021. Check the BrandMeister wiki for the latest names.
